Principal's Message

Dear Parents, Students and Well-Wishers,

It gives me immense pleasure to welcome you to Vatsalya Public School. Our school is committed to providing a safe, inclusive and stimulating learning environment where every child is encouraged to discover their potential, develop confidence and become a responsible citizen.

We believe that education is not merely about academic achievement; it is about developing good values, strong character, creativity, critical thinking and a lifelong passion for learning. Every child is unique, and our endeavour is to provide the right guidance, opportunities and encouragement for their overall development.

With the dedication of our teachers and the continuous support of parents, we strive to create a joyful and meaningful learning environment where students can learn, grow and excel.

Let us work together to nurture confident, compassionate, disciplined and responsible individuals who are prepared to meet the challenges of the future and contribute positively to society.

Warm Regards, Mrs. Gajal Joshi, Principal

Our history, vision & mission

Our Story

Vatsalya Public School is based on the Rajdhani Campus in Rohini Nagar, along Diggi Malpura Road, teaching Classes I through VIII. (Add your school's founding year and history here.)

Our Vision

To nurture confident, compassionate, creative and responsible individuals through quality education, strong values and holistic development, empowering every child to become a lifelong learner and a responsible global citizen.

Our Mission
  • To provide quality, child-centred and value-based education in a safe and inclusive environment.
  • To develop strong academic foundations along with creativity, critical thinking and problem-solving skills.
  • To nurture discipline, integrity, empathy, respect and social responsibility among students.
  • To encourage participation in sports, arts, technology and co-curricular activities for holistic development.
  • To recognise and nurture the unique talents, interests and potential of every child.
  • To promote curiosity, innovation and lifelong learning.
  • To build a strong partnership among students, teachers, parents and the community.
